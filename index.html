What We Track

Every dollar, measured.

CTR Click-Through Rate

% of people who clicked after seeing the ad. Higher = ad is relevant. 1%+ is healthy.

CPM Cost / 1,000 Views

What it costs to put your ad in front of 1,000 homeowners. Lower = efficient reach.

CPC Cost Per Click

What we pay for each click to your landing page. Lower = cheaper traffic to your quote form.

Frequency

How many times the same person sees your ad. Too high = ad fatigue. We refresh creative before that happens.

You get a weekly report showing exactly where every dollar went and what it returned — no guessing.
